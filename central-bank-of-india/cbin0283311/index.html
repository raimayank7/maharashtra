How the code CBIN0283311 is built

CBIN is the bank part: the letters of Central Bank of India. The fifth character is always the digit 0. 283311 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 423016888 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 423 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 423104), 016 is the bank and 888 is the branch.
