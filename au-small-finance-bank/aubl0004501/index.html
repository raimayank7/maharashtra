How the code AUBL0004501 is built

AUBL is the bank part: the letters of AU Small Finance Bank. The fifth character is always the digit 0. 004501 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 415765004 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 415 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 415001), 765 is the bank and 004 is the branch.
