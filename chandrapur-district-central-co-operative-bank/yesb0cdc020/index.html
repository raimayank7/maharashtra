How the code YESB0CDC020 is built

YESB is the bank part: these are the letters of Yes Bank. Chandrapur District Central Co-operative Bank sends and receives payments through that bank, so its codes carry those letters. The fifth character is always the digit 0. CDC020 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 442806503 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 442 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 442701), 806 is the bank and 503 is the branch.
