How the code ICIC00BALUC is built

ICIC is the bank part: these are the letters of ICICI Bank. Balotra Urban Co-operative Bank sends and receives payments through that bank, so its codes carry those letters. The fifth character is always the digit 0. 0BALUC is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 344808026 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 344 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 344022), 808 is the bank and 026 is the branch.
